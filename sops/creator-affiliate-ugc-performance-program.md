## 1. Purpose

This procedure explains how Ellison & Fitz onboards creators, pays affiliate commissions on sales, tests creator-made UGC in paid media, and calculates a separate paid-media performance bonus. The purpose is to give creators a transparent incentive to drive sales and make stronger creative, while keeping affiliate attribution, paid-media reporting, and payouts auditable.

> **Core rule:** A creator can earn from two separate streams in the same month. Affiliate commission is earned from attributable customer orders. UGC performance bonus is earned from eligible brand-paid media spend behind an approved creator asset. Never merge the two streams into one payout calculation.

## 2. Scope and roles

| Role | Primary responsibility | Must not do |
|---|---|---|
| Growth Lead | Approves creator offers, commission rates, UGC bonus cap, creative testing plan, and final payout file | Change a creator’s offer retroactively after work or spend has begun |
| Creator Program Manager | Onboards creators in UpPromote, maintains the creator record, issues briefs, tracks rights, and sends creator statements | Promise a bonus without the approved payout terms |
| Media Buyer | Uses the required asset naming convention, manages paid tests, exports platform data, and flags unattributable spend | Mix creator assets in a payout-eligible ad unit without recording a valid allocation method |
| Finance or Operations | Reviews finalized payout file, verifies the cap and approvals, and executes the approved non-affiliate bonus payment | Pay an unapproved estimate or a disputed amount |
| Creator | Delivers approved content, uses assigned affiliate assets, follows disclosure requirements, and reviews statements | Claim ownership of spend or performance that cannot be tied to an approved asset |

## 3. Program structure

| Stream | What earns compensation | System of record | Payout timing |
|---|---|---|---|
| Affiliate commission | A completed customer order attributed through the creator’s unique link or code | UpPromote and Shopify order data | After the configured refund and return hold period |
| UGC paid-media performance bonus | Eligible net paid-media spend delivered on an approved creator asset | Paid-ad platform report and the Creator Performance Ledger | Monthly after data is locked and approved |

The pilot begins with **affiliate-first onboarding** in UpPromote. Every approved creator receives a unique referral link and customer-facing code. Only selected candidates receive a separate UGC campaign brief and performance-bonus addendum.

The affiliate commission rate, customer discount, UGC bonus tiers, and monthly cap must be written into the creator offer before the creator begins work. The affiliate program should never be used to imply that the brand will pay a UGC performance bonus. The bonus must be described in its own addendum or campaign brief.

## 4. Required management decisions before launch

The Growth Lead must approve the following items before creators are invited. During the initial pilot, invite **5 to 10 creators** and start them as affiliate partners. Consider a creator for paid UGC only after the team has verified on-brand content quality, reliable communication, a viable product fit, and readiness to grant paid-media usage rights.

| Decision | Required choice | Approval record |
|---|---|---|
| Affiliate commission | Percentage of net eligible referred revenue, product exceptions, subscription treatment, and refund policy | Approved creator offer |
| Customer discount | Discount percentage or fixed amount, code rules, and stacking policy | Approved creator offer and UpPromote program settings |
| UGC bonus schedule | Marginal tier schedule, definition of eligible media spend, and the monthly cap | UGC performance addendum |
| Pilot cap | Select one firm cap per creator per month. Do not state a range. | Approved creator offer |
| Rights | Ad platforms, paid-usage duration, editing permission, whitelisting or Spark Ads status, and renewal terms | Rights addendum or campaign brief |
| Reporting cadence | Weekly estimated scorecard and monthly final statement | Campaign brief |
| Creator selection | Affiliate-first for the pilot; paid UGC is invitation-only after content and rights review | Creator Master List and campaign brief |
| Internal operating record | Post status, approvals, and blockers using the standard labels in #ef-creator-ops | Slack operations Canvas |

### Current proposed UGC performance schedule

Use this schedule only after the Growth Lead selects the final monthly cap for the creator or campaign.

| Tier | Eligible monthly paid-media spend per creator | Marginal bonus rate |
|---|---:|---:|
| Tier 1 | First $10,000 | 10.0% |
| Tier 2 | Next $20,000, from $10,000 to $30,000 | 5.0% |
| Tier 3 | Next $40,000, from $30,000 to $70,000 | 2.5% |
| Tier 4 | Above $70,000 | 1.0% |

The schedule is **marginal**, not retroactive. A creator who reaches $30,000 in eligible spend earns $1,000 from Tier 1 plus $1,000 from Tier 2, for a $2,000 gross UGC bonus before the approved monthly cap. The calculation must use the payout formula in Section 10.

## 5. Creator onboarding procedure

### 5.1 Create the creator record

Before an affiliate invite is sent, create one row in the Creator Master List. The creator record is the permanent reference point for the partnership.

| Required field | Standard |
|---|---|
| Creator ID | `CREATOR_FIRSTNAME_01`, for example `CREATOR_JANE_01` |
| Legal name and business name | Required for payment and agreement workflow |
| Social handles | Include platform and direct profile URL |
| Email | Use the email that will receive UpPromote invitations and statements |
| Affiliate code | Short, readable, unique, and checked for conflicts |
| Affiliate link | Created in UpPromote and copied into the record |
| UGC eligibility | `Affiliate only`, `Affiliate + UGC approved`, or `UGC paused` |
| Rights status | `Not granted`, `Active`, `Expiring`, or `Expired` |
| UGC cap | Exact approved amount, never a range |
| Start and renewal date | Used for rights and offer review |

### 5.2 Configure UpPromote

The Creator Program Manager configures the creator’s affiliate program in UpPromote using the approved offer. At minimum, each creator receives a unique link and a unique discount code so that both link-led and code-led sales can be attributed. The individual creator’s UpPromote portal remains the source of truth for their affiliate activity and commission status.

Use the following operating standards.

| UpPromote setting | Required pilot standard |
|---|---|
| Registration | Invite approved creators directly during the pilot. Do not open public enrollment until the review process is documented. |
| Approval | Manual approval required. |
| Affiliate link | One unique link per creator. |
| Affiliate code | One unique, readable code per creator. |
| Commission basis | Net eligible product revenue after discounts, cancellations, and refunds, unless an approved offer states otherwise. |
| Commission rate | Use the creator’s approved affiliate offer. |
| Commission hold | Match the return and refund window selected by the Growth Lead. |
| Payout | Follow UpPromote’s approved payment workflow. Do not include UGC paid-media bonuses in an affiliate commission payout unless finance has explicitly approved the combined payment method. |
| Fraud controls | Review self-referrals, code leakage, duplicate orders, and suspicious order patterns before approval. |

### 5.3 Send the onboarding package

Send the creator a concise onboarding package containing the affiliate offer, unique link, unique code, creative expectations, rights terms, disclosure expectations, and the UGC bonus addendum if the creator is eligible for paid-media testing.

#### Creator onboarding message template

If we select one of your approved UGC assets for paid media, any separate performance bonus is governed by the UGC performance terms we provided. ## 6. UGC briefing, delivery, and rights approval

The Creator Program Manager sends a written brief before the creator begins UGC production. The brief must define product, message, creative angle, deliverable count, format, creator disclosure, submission date, revision policy, and paid-usage rights.

No asset may enter paid testing until all three requirements are complete: content approval, asset ID assignment, and rights confirmation.

| Requirement | Evidence required |
|---|---|
| Content approval | Written approval in the campaign record or approved project-management task |
| Paid-usage rights | Creator agreement or addendum with dates, platforms, edit rights, and any whitelisting permissions |
| Asset ID | Unique asset ID added to the file, approval log, and ad naming convention |

## 7. Asset identification and naming convention

Every payout-eligible creator asset must have a durable creator ID and asset ID. Do not use vague filenames such as `final_v2.mp4`.

| Object | Required format | Example |
|---|---|---|
| Creator ID | `CREATOR_FIRSTNAME_01` | `CREATOR_JANE_01` |
| Source asset ID | `UGC_FIRSTNAME_###` | `UGC_JANE_001` |
| Asset version | Add `_V##` | `UGC_JANE_001_V03` |
| Paid-ad name | Retain asset ID plus concept, hook, format, and version | `ugc_jane_001_testimonial_hook01_vid_v01` |
| Payout ledger row | Asset ID, platform, date period, and ad ID | `UGC_JANE_001 | Meta | 2026-08` |

The asset ID must appear in the source file name, asset approval log, platform ad name, creative testing log, and Creator Performance Ledger. This is the minimum evidence needed to trace eligible spend back to the correct creator.

## 8. Paid-media testing rules

The Media Buyer must use a controlled testing structure that allows spend to be attributed to an individual creator asset.

### 8.1 Eligible spend

Eligible spend is net media spend actually delivered on an approved creator asset during the creator’s active rights period. It must be supported by the final reporting export from the applicable ad platform.

### 8.2 Ineligible spend

Do not include the following in a creator’s bonus calculation unless the Growth Lead creates a written exception.

| Ineligible category | Reason |
|---|---|
| Taxes, agency fees, platform credits, and production costs | Not paid media spend delivered on the creator’s asset |
| Spend outside the active rights period | The brand is not authorized to use the asset for the calculation period |
| Spend after the creator reaches the approved monthly cap | The cap controls maximum liability |
| Mixed-creator dynamic creative without a documented allocation method | Spend cannot be reliably attributed to one creator |
| Spend linked to an unapproved or materially edited asset without a new asset version | Rights and attribution record are incomplete |
| Suspicious, duplicated, or reporting-error spend | Must be investigated before a creator statement is issued |

### 8.3 Dynamic creative and mixed assets

Do not place multiple creators in one payout-eligible dynamic creative or flexible-creative unit unless the platform can produce a reliable, documented asset-level spend allocation. If reliable allocation is unavailable, classify the spend as not bonus-eligible. This prevents payout disputes and maintains a defensible process.

## 9. Creator Performance Ledger

Maintain one shared ledger owned by the Creator Program Manager. The ledger may be a protected spreadsheet during the pilot. It must be updated weekly and locked at month end.

| Field | Description |
|---|---|
| Month | Reporting month in `YYYY-MM` format |
| Creator ID | Stable creator ID |
| Asset ID | Unique creator asset version |
| Platform | Meta, TikTok, or other approved platform |
| Campaign and ad ID | Platform identifiers used to validate spend |
| Rights status | Active, expiring, expired, or exception approved |
| Eligible spend | Net delivered, attributable bonus-eligible spend |
| Estimated payout | Preliminary tier calculation, clearly marked estimated |
| Final payout | Locked amount after approvals and cap |
| Affiliate revenue | Separately reported from UpPromote, not added to UGC spend |
| Affiliate commission | Separately reported from UpPromote |
| Statement status | Draft, reviewed, approved, paid, or disputed |
| Evidence link | Link to platform export, ad screenshot, or reporting source |

## 10. Monthly UGC performance-bonus calculation

At the end of each month, the Creator Program Manager calculates the creator’s UGC performance bonus from total eligible spend across all approved creator assets. The formula is marginal and subject to the creator’s approved monthly cap.

```text
gross_bonus =
  10% × min(S, $10,000)
+ 5% × min(max(S − $10,000, 0), $20,000)
+ 2.5% × min(max(S − $30,000, 0), $40,000)
+ 1% × max(S − $70,000, 0)

final_bonus = min(approved_monthly_cap, gross_bonus)
```

Where `S` equals eligible monthly paid-media spend attributable to that creator. The Creator Performance Ledger must show the spend total, tier-by-tier calculation, cap, and final payment amount.

| Eligible monthly spend | Example gross bonus |
|---:|---:|
| $10,000 | $1,000 |
| $30,000 | $2,000 |
| $70,000 | $3,000 |
| $100,000 | $3,300 |
| $170,000 | $4,000 |
| $270,000 | $5,000 |

## 11. Weekly creator scorecard

The Creator Program Manager sends a short, read-only scorecard on the same weekday each week. The scorecard is motivational but is not a payment promise.

Every weekly scorecard must state: **“Estimated through [date]. Final UGC performance bonus is calculated after month-end reporting, eligibility review, rights verification, and the approved cap.”**

| Scorecard field | Include? | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| Creator’s approved assets and status | Yes | Show only their own assets |
| Estimated eligible spend | Yes | Mark as estimated |
| Estimated current tier progress | Yes | Show current tier and estimated bonus |
| Final affiliate earnings | Yes, through UpPromote or a separate summary | Subject to affiliate hold rules |
| Asset-level CPA or ROAS | Optional | Show only if measurement is valid and approved for creator sharing |
| Other creators’ performance | No | Confidential |
| Total ad-account budget or margin | No | Confidential |

## 12. Month-end close and payout approval

The month-end close must be completed before a UGC performance bonus is paid.

1. The Media Buyer exports final platform data for the closed month and checks the asset IDs against the ad naming convention.
2. The Creator Program Manager removes ineligible spend, verifies rights status, totals eligible spend by creator, and calculates the gross bonus and cap.
3. The Growth Lead reviews all bonuses, exceptions, unattributable spend, and disputes.
4. Finance or Operations verifies the approved payout file and pays the UGC performance bonus as a separate line item from affiliate commission whenever practical.
5. The Creator Program Manager sends the final statement and changes the ledger status to `Paid` after payment confirmation.

### Final statement template

```text
Hi [Creator First Name],

Your [Month] creator statement is finalized.

Affiliate commission: [AMOUNT]
UGC performance bonus: [AMOUNT]
Eligible paid-media spend used for the UGC calculation: [AMOUNT]
Approved monthly UGC cap: [AMOUNT]

The UGC performance bonus reflects approved asset-level spend, the program tiers, and the terms in your creator agreement. The payment status is: [STATUS].

Thank you for creating with Ellison & Fitz.
```

## 13. Escalation rules

| Situation | Required action |
|---|---|
| Creator asks to change commission rate or monthly cap after work begins | Escalate to Growth Lead. Do not promise a change. |
| Asset is used after rights expire | Pause the asset, document the issue, and escalate to Growth Lead. |
| Spend cannot be tied to an approved creator asset | Mark spend ineligible until a documented allocation is approved. |
| Creator disputes the bonus | Freeze only the disputed UGC performance amount, retain evidence, and escalate within two business days. |
| Code leakage, self-referral, or suspicious affiliate orders | Hold affiliate commission approval and investigate in UpPromote and Shopify. |
| Creator requests live access to the paid-ad account | Decline. Provide the approved creator scorecard instead. |
| The creator hits the monthly cap | Continue or pause media only after Growth Lead decides whether the additional spend has strategic value without incremental bonus liability. |

## 14. Creator lifecycle and product-rotation workflow

The program runs **affiliate first, product specific, and evidence based**. Every candidate begins in the Creator Pipeline before an offer is made. The team should select one primary product for each creator at a time, issue one brief, assess the resulting organic content and affiliate signal, and only then decide whether that creator is a suitable candidate for paid UGC.

| Stage | Internal action | Exit condition |
|---|---|---|
| Sourced | Create a Creator Pipeline row with source, portfolio or social link, basic audience note, and owner. | Basic candidate record is complete. |
| Screening | Review content quality, brand fit, audience relevance, reliability signals, and product fit. Request current public social links, two or three short-form examples, audience summary, and availability. | Growth Lead marks `Advance` or `Decline`. |
| Activated | Approve the affiliate offer, create the UpPromote invitation, and issue one unique link and code. | Creator confirms portal access and affiliate assets. |
| Briefed | Assign one product brief from the Product Queue and record the brief ID, due date, and owner. | Creator acknowledges the brief. |
| Live | Review the published content, disclosure, link or code use, and initial affiliate signal. | Creator is retained as affiliate-only or considered for UGC. |
| UGC candidate | Evaluate the creator only after content quality, reliability, product fit, and rights readiness are verified. | Separate UGC brief and rights addendum are approved or the creator remains affiliate-only. |

### 14.1 Product Queue roles

| Role | Definition | Assignment rule |
|---|---|---|
| Primary affiliate spotlight | Current best product for routine affiliate outreach. | Use as the default only when it fits the creator's audience and content style. |
| Test product | Product requiring further creator-market-fit validation. | Use only with a deliberately selected creator segment. |
| Hold | Product that is not currently receiving new creator briefs. | Do not issue new briefs. |
| Retired | Product no longer eligible for creator promotion. | Archive existing brief and remove from offers. |

The Growth Lead reviews the Product Queue weekly. Product role can change without changing the overall affiliate program. Each creator receives **one primary product brief at a time** to keep content focused and learn which creator, product, and angle perform together.

### 14.2 Current primary affiliate spotlight: Ellison Fluid Pant

The Ellison Fluid Pant is the initial primary affiliate spotlight. The live product page should remain the source of truth for current price, inventory, and other dynamic information. The approved product truths are: lightweight breathable striped fabric, relaxed straight-leg cut, adjustable drawstring waist, machine washability, and quick-drying care.[1]

The default affiliate brief should direct creators to make one vertical short-form video or one feed post that uses their assigned link and code, tags `@ellisonandfitz`, uses the applicable disclosure, and provides the published URL to the creator team. Approved angles include movement and fit, travel or day-to-night styling, one pant in three ways, and a specific honest product observation. The affiliate brief does **not** grant paid-media usage rights or promise a UGC performance bonus.

### 14.3 Internal and external communication rule

`#ef-creator-ops` remains internal only. The channel coordinates screening, offer approval, UpPromote activation, briefs, asset approval, and payout decisions. Creator communication occurs through email, UpPromote, or an approved direct-message channel. Do not post personal payment data, agreements, detailed ad-account exports, or private offer terms in Slack.

## 15. Pilot success criteria

Run the initial pilot with 5 to 10 approved creators for at least 30 to 60 days. Evaluate the system only after two clean monthly closes if the timing allows.

| Measure | Review question |
|---|---|
| Affiliate activity | Are creators generating tracked clicks, orders, and net revenue through links or codes? |
| UGC delivery | Are creators providing usable assets on time and within the brief? |
| Paid-media performance | Which asset IDs can sustain spend with acceptable CPA, ROAS, and creative refresh performance? |
| Operations load | How many hours are spent on onboarding, content collection, reporting, and payout reconciliation? |
| Payout accuracy | Were any statements disputed, corrected, or delayed? |
| Economics | Does creator cost, including affiliate commission and UGC bonus, support target contribution margin? |

Move to a larger creator-management platform only when manual operations are demonstrably the bottleneck or when a vendor can show a working configuration for asset-level paid-media spend, the exact marginal tier logic, cap enforcement, and creator-specific reporting.

## 17. Operating checklist

### Before inviting a creator

- [ ] Creator offer approved, including affiliate commission, customer code, UGC eligibility, and exact cap.
- [ ] The Creator Master List records the exact affiliate rate, customer discount, commission hold, selected UGC cap, and rights status. Do not leave any commercial term as a range.
- [ ] Internal owner posts a `[CREATOR]` update in #ef-creator-ops with stage, next action, and blocker status.
- [ ] Creator record created with unique creator ID.
- [ ] UpPromote link and code prepared.
- [ ] Rights and UGC performance terms ready, if applicable.

### Before spending paid media on an asset

- [ ] Creator asset approved.
- [ ] Rights are active for platform, duration, and required editing or whitelisting use.
- [ ] Asset ID and version assigned.
- [ ] Asset ID appears in file name, approval log, and paid-ad name.
- [ ] Test structure supports creator-asset attribution.

### Before paying a UGC performance bonus

- [ ] Month is closed and data export is final.
- [ ] Eligible spend is documented by creator and asset.
- [ ] Rights status and cap are verified.
- [ ] Tier calculation is reviewed by Growth Lead.
- [ ] Finance or Operations approves the final payout file.
- [ ] Final statement sent and ledger status updated.
