Routing rule

1
Cherry/Beehiveship is the default for every product. Work her through WhatsApp "cherry – supplier 4" and Slack #08-cherry-beehive.
2
Fluid Pant is Cherry only. Never send Fluid Pant to Hart or Lay.
3
When Cherry cannot source an item, or cannot match the live listing in color or style, Abby asks Hart and/or Lay before refunding.
  • Hart: WhatsApp "Hart – Wio supplier 1". Payment is the Wiio balance.
  • Lay: WhatsApp "Lay – Supplier 3". He is paid from a prepaid balance that Kevin tops up. Do not use #07-lay-dropsure; that channel is dead.
4
The substitute must be an exact match to the live listing. A "similar" item ships only with Kevin's explicit OK. If there is no exact match and Kevin has not approved a similar, Abby offers a swap, store credit, or refund per the CS SOP.
5
Check Meta ads before drafting a product or removing a color. Do not set a running ad product to Draft without Kevin's OK.

🚨 Double-ship (Redo vs Cherry/Beehive)
🚨
Live risk: Redo Salt Lake accepts or ships a pant while Beehiveship is still pending or paid.
1
If Redo has accepted or shipped and Cherry/Beehiveship is still pending or paid, cancel Cherry in #08-cherry-beehive.
2
Do not re-ping Cherry.
3
Do not wait for two tracking numbers. Beehive dummy TX tracking on the x-redo line is a false positive. x-redo is the Redo protection line, not a second pant.
4
Two Shopify Flow workflows are live: Hold Beehive when Redo has the item, and Catch Redo accept. When Redo is assigned or accepts, Flow tags redo-accepted and posts to Slack #04-ops-alerts.
